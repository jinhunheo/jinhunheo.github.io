## The problem

The car's engine sits at the rear, so the throttle cable has to travel from the
driver's foot through the chassis without fouling anything mid-car. The pedal also has
to meet competition rules, and it needed a way to return on its own.

## What I designed

- The throttle pedal assembly in SolidWorks, with a custom throttle cable linkage
  routed around the rear-engine layout without interfering with mid-chassis components.
- A 9 in pedal arm, 25% shorter than the industry standard, while still meeting
  competition regulations.
- A spring return mechanism, so the pedal comes back reliably and the driver can feel
  it.
